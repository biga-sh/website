---
title: Introduction
description: biga broadcasts a folder as a live, read-only link. No accounts, and nothing for viewers to install.
---

biga streams the files you are working on to a browser tab. You run one
command in a project, share the link it prints, and anyone who opens it
follows your saves as they happen: a file tree, syntax highlighting, and a
timeline they can step back through.

A screen share sends pixels. biga sends the files themselves, so viewers can
select, search and scroll at their own pace, and nothing is transmitted while
you are not saving.

## Where to start

<Steps>
<Step>

[Install the CLI](/docs/installation), or follow the [quickstart](/docs/quickstart) to go from nothing to a shared link in two commands.

</Step>
<Step>

Read [what gets shared](/docs/what-gets-shared) before you broadcast a project that holds secrets. Credentials are excluded by default, but it is worth knowing exactly how.

</Step>

</Step>
